🧭 Compass Calibration Guide

  • Hold Phone Flat: Keep your mobile device completely flat parallel to the ground for optimal magnetic sensor readings.
  • Figure-8 Motion: Wave your phone slowly in a horizontal "8" pattern 2-3 times in the air to calibrate the built-in sensor.
  • Avoid Magnetic Cases: Remove phone cases containing magnets, metallic rings, or magnetic latches.
  • Stay Away from Electronics: Move away from metal tables, laptops, or large electrical appliances.
