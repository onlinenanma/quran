2. Information We Handle

Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us by email (appnanma@gmail.com) or through a contact form, you may share your name, email address, subject, and message content. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or confidential information in messages.

Technical & Connection Data

When you visit a website or load static assets and media over the internet, your browser or device naturally transmits standard technical details—such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, and requested URLs—to the servers or content delivery networks serving those files so pages and audio can load properly.

3. Local Storage, Offline Caching & Text-to-Speech

  • Browser Storage & Service Workers: If the site or Progressive Web App (PWA) uses localStorage, session storage, or Service Worker caching, those files stay on your own device to remember preferences, improve load speed, or support offline access. You can clear them anytime through your browser or device settings.
  • Audio & Speech Synthesis: Audio files or browser Text-to-Speech (TTS) features may rely on hosted audio files or voices provided by your browser or operating system. Speech synthesis is typically processed on your device or via your browser's speech engine according to its own settings.
  • Screen Wake Lock: If a reading or audio feature keeps your screen awake during active playback, it only runs while you use that feature and can be controlled or stopped by you.
